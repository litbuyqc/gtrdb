The quick answer

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. A useful list records the product, source link, selected variant, size information and date checked. Treat each entry as a starting point: verify the current listing before ordering. This page offers a browsing guide rather than a downloadable sheet or live inventory feed.

03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

Turn Oopbuy finds into a useful shortlist.

An Oopbuy product list works best when every saved item answers the same basic questions. Keep your comparison notes specific enough to revisit later.

  1. Choose a category and a purpose.

    Begin with what you need: everyday shoes, a layering piece, training wear or an accessory. Set a budget before collecting alternatives.

  2. Save the exact product and variant.

    Record the source URL, seller name, color, size and date checked. Similar photographs can appear on different listings, so an image alone is not a reliable identifier.

  3. Compare fit and supporting details.

    Read measurements, fabric descriptions and return conditions. If inspection photos are available, compare them with the selected variant and ask about unclear details before proceeding.

  4. Review the full cost before payment.

    Separate the item price from any delivery, service, shipping, conversion and import charges. Confirm which charges apply to your destination and order.

05 / Clear answers

Before you follow a find.

Quick answers about spreadsheet browsing, product checks and this independent guide.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not operated by or endorsed by Oopbuy or the fashion brands shown.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides category links and a comparison guide. It does not offer a downloadable spreadsheet, live inventory or an Oopbuy account integration.

How should I check sizing before ordering?

Compare the listing’s measurements with a similar item you own. For clothing, compare chest width, length and sleeve or inseam measurements. For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and stated foot-length guidance.

Does a product photo prove authenticity or availability?

No. A reference image does not establish seller authorization, authenticity, stock or an exact match to a linked listing. Check the actual seller, product description, supporting documentation and return conditions.

What costs should I compare beyond the item price?

Compare any applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion charges and import taxes. Costs depend on the seller, service, parcel and destination; confirm them before payment.

How can I keep an Oopbuy product list useful?

Save exact source links, record variants and measurements, and add a date to each check. Mark missing details and remove unavailable entries when you revisit your shortlist.
